## Product Purpose
Connect volunteer engagement to mission-aligned opportunities. The supplied reference prioritizes actions and outcomes over CRM administration.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Overview includes 35 volunteers, 42% return rate and 6 opportunities, explicitly illustrative. College Corps exploration explains funded people, campus partnership and supervision requirements. Outreach is a local editable draft with a consent-aware audience and no message delivery. People, Funding and Events are navigable demo views. No live AI, personal records or financial actions.

## Brand Commitments

## Evidence on Hand
